Everyone on the team shares one board. **Not Started** comes first and **Done** last, always; the columns between are your team's own. Changes show up for others within a few seconds.

## Cards

- **Add a card**: type its title above the columns and choose **Add card**. It goes into **Not Started**. From Slack or Discord, type `/gb-add` and the card.
- **Move a card**: drag it to another column or up and down in one. On a phone, press and hold a card, then drag.
- **Put yourself on a card**: choose **Add me** on it. Everyone sees who's on each card; **Remove me** takes you off.
- **Put someone else on a card** (mentors, admins and trusted students): open the card and pick them under **Add someone…**; the × beside a name takes them off. Trusted students are chosen on the Admin page ([Set up Planner](setup#trusted-students)).
- **Rename or move a card**: click it.
- **Delete a card** (admins only): choose the trash can beside **Add me** on it. It's deleted for everyone and can't be undone.
- With a keyboard, **Space** picks up a card, the arrow keys move it and **Space** puts it down; **Enter** opens it.

## Columns

The columns between Not Started and Done are set up by mentors and admins on Planner's Admin page ([Set up Planner](setup#columns)). Until there are any, the board shows "Add columns in the Admin panel" where they'd be.

## Meeting overviews

Mentors and admins choose **Send meeting overview** to post the board to the team's chat, in the channel chosen on the Admin page ([Set up Planner](setup)). The message is headed with the day it was sent and has two lists, **Not Started** and **Started** (every card in the columns between Not Started and Done), with who's on each card, and keeps itself up to date: a card that's finished is crossed off where it was, new ones appear, and deleted ones go. Sending another overview starts a new message; the old one stops updating.
