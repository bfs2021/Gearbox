Planner is a board your whole team shares: add a card to **Not Started** for each thing that needs doing, and drag it from **Not Started** to **In Progress** to **Done**. Everyone sees the same board, and changes show up for others within a few seconds. Members can also add cards from Slack or Discord with `/gb-add`, and mentors can post a meeting overview that keeps itself up to date.

## Who does what

- **Everyone signed in** adds, renames and moves cards, and puts themselves on a card or takes themselves off.
- **Mentors and admins** send meeting overviews to the team's chat and choose their channel.
- **Admins** also delete cards.
- **Kiosks** can look at the board but not change it.
