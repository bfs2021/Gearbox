# G3 Planner (the team's board of cards)

- Not released yet. `apps/planner` is a plugin-based UI like Schedule's (the shared top bar and theme) with two pages, **Board** (`/`, `src/plugins/board/`) and **Admin** (`/admin`, mentors and admins: the columns, the overview channel and trusted students; `apps/planner/README.md`); `workers/planner` (Hono, Drizzle, D1 `PLANNER_DB`) has the API below.
- **What it holds** (migration `0002`, which dropped `0001`'s empty placeholder; `src/db/schema.ts`): `cards`, each with a title (1–200 characters), a `status` and a `sort_order` within its column (gaps are fine; a move renumbers the column it lands in).
- **Columns** (migration `0006`, `src/lib/columns.ts`, `src/routes/columns.ts`): **Not Started and Done always stay**; between them are the team's own (`board_columns`: a name, 1–40 characters, unique in the team and never "Not Started" or "Done"; left to right by `sort_order`; at most 20). A card in one has status `in_progress` and its `column_id`; Not Started and Done cards have none. The API names a column `"not_started"`, `"done"` or a middle column's id (`Column`; cards answer `column`, never `status`). Mentors and admins arrange them on the Admin page (`apps/planner/src/plugins/admin/columns-card.tsx`; the board has no column controls, and shows "Add columns in the Admin panel" where they'd be while there are none, `no-columns.tsx`): add (`POST /columns`, at the right end), rename (`PATCH /columns/:id`), reorder (`PUT /columns/order { ids }`: every one, once) and delete (`DELETE /columns/:id`: its cards go to the bottom of Not Started, in order, in the same batch). A team starts with "In Progress" (`seedColumns`, the manifest's `seed` hook, `POST /internal/teams/:id/seed`: only when it has no columns); `0006` made one for every team already using Planner and put its In Progress cards in it. `card_members` (migration `0003`): who's on each card, by G3ID user id, deleted with its card (foreign key, cascading). No dates or anything else yet.
- **Who changes it:** everyone signed in reads the board; everyone but a kiosk (`requireOAuthSession`) adds, renames and moves cards, and puts themselves on a card or takes themselves off. **Mentors, admins and trusted students put anyone on a card or take them off** (`canAssign`/`requireAssigner` in `src/lib/trusted.ts`; `/me`'s `canAssign`; the card dialog's `AssignPeople`, searching `GET /cards/people` with `PersonPicker` from `@g3/ui`, saved at once). Trusted students (migration `0007`, `trusted_students`) are chosen by mentors and admins in the Admin page's Trusted students card (`src/routes/trusted.ts`: `GET /trusted` gives `{ trusted, students }`, the students not yet trusted; `PUT|DELETE /trusted/:userId`, only the team's active members). **Only G3ID admins delete cards** (`requireAdmin`; `/me`'s `isAdmin` shows the trash button beside Add me on each card, and Delete in a card's dialog). `/me` returns `canEdit` (false for a kiosk), which hides the page's controls.
- **API** (`src/routes/cards.ts`): `GET /board` (`{ columns, cards }` in one answer: what the page polls), `GET /cards` (every card, with `memberIds`), `GET /cards/people` (the team's active members' names, from G3ID), `POST /cards { title }` (always at the bottom of Not Started, where every card starts; 201), `PATCH /cards/:id { title }`, `PUT /cards/:id/move { column, index }` (into that column at `index`, 0 the top, past the end the bottom; a middle column must be the team's), `PUT|DELETE /cards/:id/members/me` (the caller on or off; twice is still once), `PUT|DELETE /cards/:id/members/:userId` (anyone on or off: mentors, admins and trusted students; only the team's active members go on), `DELETE /cards/:id` (admins only).
- **Meeting overviews** (`src/lib/overview.ts`, migrations `0004`, `0005` and `0008`): mentors and admins choose a channel on the Admin page (`/admin`, `apps/planner/src/plugins/admin/`; `GET`/`PUT /settings { overviewChannel }`, `planner_settings`, one row per team, logged with `logTeamChange`) and choose **Send meeting overview** on the board (`POST /overviews`, in `DEMO_BLOCKED`; `GET /overviews/latest` for "Last sent"). It's posted with `postTeamMessage` and kept up to date with `editTeamMessage` (`@g3/auth`, through G3ID): after every change to the board (`afterChange` in `src/lib/cards.ts`, in `waitUntil`, and after `/gb-add`) `updateOverview` rebuilds the team's **latest** overview's text (`overviewText`, a pure function) and edits it only if the text changed (`overviews.last_text`). Its heading has the day it was sent ("📋 Meeting overview · Tuesday, November 17, 2026", `overviewDate`), in the sender's time zone: the page sends its browser's with `POST /overviews { timeZone }` (`parseTimeZone`; UTC without), kept in `overviews.time_zone` (migration `0008`), so edits keep the same day. It has two lists, **Not Started** and **Started** (every middle column, left to right), with who's on each card; every card listed is remembered with the list it was last in (`overview_cards.section`, migration `0005`), so one moved to Done since stays there crossed off (`~~…~~`), after the open ones, and a deleted one goes. No Done list. Titles and names go through `plainChatText` from `@g3/slack`. It always fits one Discord message (1,900 characters; long sections end "…and N more"). An edit that fails is logged and the next change tries again. Older overviews stop updating.
- **`/gb-add`** (`CHAT_COMMANDS` in `@g3/auth`): `POST /internal/teams/:teamId/commands/gb-add { userId, text }` from the platform, after G3ID found the member from their Slack or Discord; adds the card at the bottom of Not Started under their name (`addCard`) and answers `{ reply }`. Setup on Slack and Discord: `docs/deploy.md`, "Slash commands".
- **Syncing:** the page polls `GET /cards` every 5 seconds while it's visible, and after each change, skipping a poll during a drag or while a change is on its way, so an old answer never undoes what's on screen. No live push (that would take Durable Objects, a new Cloudflare product: ask the owners first). Two people moving cards in the same column at once: the last move wins, and the next poll shows everyone the result.
- Ports: app 5188, worker 8799 (inspector 9241). Icon: a clipboard with a checklist (`apps/planner/public/favicon.svg`, also `apps/portal/src/assets/app-icons/planner.svg` and the platform's docs icon), with the PNGs and `.ico` drawn from the same shapes.
- In the app library for every team, with a `preview` warning (`src/manifest.ts`); not switched on for any team (no platform migration; switch it on by hand in dev), and not on the platform's public home page or the issue forms' app list yet.
- **On Cloudflare**: production's D1 (`g3-planner-prod`) and staging's are created, their ids in `wrangler.toml` and `infra/staging/resources.json`; migrations run with each deploy (`deploy.yml`).
- Text boxes use the demo's `task` and `column` kinds (`packages/demo/src/safe-text.ts`). The demo's board (`src/demo.ts`) has two middle columns (In Progress, Testing) and a few cards in each column.
- Team-scoped: every table carries `team_id`, and every query goes through `inTeam`/`withTeam` (in the tenancy lint). It has delete, move, export and demo hooks (the platform's `PLANNER` binding, `TEAM_TABLES` in the schema, children first) and `test/isolation.test.ts`. `test/columns.test.ts` covers the columns; `test/trusted.test.ts` trusted students and putting others on cards; `test/cards.test.ts` covers adding, moving, renaming, deleting, who's on a card and kiosks; `test/overview.test.ts` the overview's text, the channel, sending, updating and `/gb-add`. Who's on a card, and the trusted students, are in the privacy policy's app table. A new table goes in the tenancy lint, `TEAM_TABLES` and the isolation test's `TABLES`; a new route that takes a body gets one in the isolation test's `bodies`.
